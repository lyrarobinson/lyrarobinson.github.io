Proposal render for soil as an installation. The live simulation is projected across a darkened room, with dishes of ground gathered from the surrounding site on a lit plinth. Moisture and temperature readings from the samples feed back into the simulation, changing the conditions the worms have to navigate.

Below are images from the current working simulation. The worms move through a 2D model of soil containing bacterial colonies and diffusing chemical traces. These gradients stimulate sensory neurons in the C. elegans connectome and shape how the worms move through the environment. The different views also show activity moving through the neural network as the simulation runs.
